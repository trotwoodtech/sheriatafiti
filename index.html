Areas of Practice

Four disciplines, one practice.

Each engagement draws on the same underlying expertise: rigorous legal research applied to real transactions and real communities.

§1 — Legal Advisory

Legal Counsel & Litigation Support

Regulatory advisory, contract drafting, compliance audits, and representation before Kenyan courts and tribunals.

§2 — Carbon Markets

Carbon Investments

Structuring, due diligence, and legal risk assessment for voluntary and compliance carbon projects.

§3 — Environmental Law

EIA & ESIA

Environmental and social impact assessment support, NEMA licensing, and mitigation planning.

Benefit-Sharing & FPIC

Community consultation design, free prior and informed consent processes, and benefit-sharing agreements.

Founder

Advocate Charles Kipruto Kiprono

Sheria Tafiti is founded and led by Charles Kipruto Kiprono, LLB, an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya. His practice sits at the crossing point of environmental law and climate finance — advising on the legal architecture that determines whether carbon and conservation projects hold up in practice, not only on paper.
