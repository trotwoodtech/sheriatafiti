Services

Three practice groups, one integrated approach.

Legal advisory, carbon investment structuring, and environmental impact assessment — engaged separately or together.

§1

Legal Advisory

Regulatory Compliance

Advisory on Kenyan environmental, land, and investment law, including licensing pathways and regulatory risk assessment.

Contract & Transaction Support

Drafting and negotiation support for commercial agreements, land transactions, and cross-border investment contracts.

Legal Audits

Structured review of an organisation's legal exposure, documentation, and compliance posture.

Litigation Support

Research, drafting, and representation before the High Court and relevant tribunals.

§2

Carbon Investments

Project Structuring

Legal architecture for voluntary and compliance carbon projects, including entity structuring and land-tenure verification.

Investor Due Diligence

Legal risk assessment for parties investing in or acquiring carbon credits and offset projects.

Benefit-Sharing Agreements

Drafting agreements that distribute carbon project revenue between developers, government, and host communities.

Registry & Standards Compliance

Advisory on alignment with Verra, Gold Standard, and Kenyan carbon market regulatory requirements.

§3

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA / ESIA)

EIA / ESIA Coordination

End-to-end coordination of environmental and social impact assessments, from scoping to NEMA submission.

NEMA Licensing

Preparation and management of licence applications and regulatory correspondence with the National Environment Management Authority.

Community Consultation

Design and facilitation of stakeholder engagement and free, prior and informed consent (FPIC) processes.

Mitigation & Monitoring Plans

Development of environmental and social management plans and post-approval monitoring frameworks.
